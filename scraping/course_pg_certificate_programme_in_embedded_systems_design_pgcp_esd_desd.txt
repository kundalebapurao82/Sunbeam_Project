Overview
Syllabus
What You'll Learn
Candidates will be trained in hardware and firmware design of Embedded System based applications, Internet of Things (IoT), project development and management skills. At the end of the course students will be able to work as System Design Engineers and Embedded Engineers with good knowledge of RT Linux, Microcontroller and Microprocessor based design, Device Drivers and RTOS. Students can start a career as Embedded Developer, Tester and Lead Project Manager after gaining relevant experience.
Course Description
Embedded systems is a specialized field where engineers need to have good knowledge in hardware and software design. Keeping this aspect in mind, C-DAC has designed this course with equal emphasis on hardware and software, enabling engineers to meet the challenges in designing and developing cutting-edge embedded systems. The objective of this course is to enable students to work on embedded technology scenarios, as well as prepare them to keep pace with the changing face of technology and the requirements of the growing electronics and IT industry. The curriculum has been designed keeping in mind the latest technology in embedded systems as well as the contemporary and future human resource requirements of the ICT industry. The course includes a module on Internet of Things (IoT), specifically focusing on IoT architecture, applications, standards and protocols.
