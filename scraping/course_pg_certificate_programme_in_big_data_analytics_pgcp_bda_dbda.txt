Post Graduate Certificate Programme in Big Data Analytics (PGCP-BDA)
The PGCP-BDA will educate candidates who want to make an impact in the corporate and academic world in the field of Big Data Analytics as Data Scientists and Researchers, Big Data Leads/Investigators/Managers, Business Analysts and Data Visualization Specialists.

Overview
Syllabus
What You'll Learn
After completing this course, students will be trained in Statistics and Machine Learning using Python. They will make data driven decisions which provide them a competitive advantage in the market, technologies like Hadoop, Spark, Hive, Machine Learning provides a spring board for AI which makes them ready for Industry 4.0. At the end of the course, students will be able to work as data analysts, data engineers. Studying Big Data will broaden their horizon by surpassing market forecast / predictions for Big Data Analytics.
Course Description
This course is suitable for those already working in analytics to enhance their theoretical and conceptual knowledge, as well as those with analytical aptitude who want to start a career in big data analytics across various business sectors. Students will be able to work with big data platforms, analyze various big data analysis techniques for useful business applications, design efficient algorithms for mining data from large volumes, analyze Hadoop and Map Reduce techniques associated with big data analytics, and explore big data applications
The Postgraduate Certificate Programme in Big Data Analytics (PGCP-BDA) blends theory and practical focus on the following:
Explore the fundamental concepts of Big Data Analytics with in-depth knowledge and understanding of the Big Data Analytics domain
Understanding different search methods and visualization techniques and using different techniques for data stream mining
To analyze and solve conceptual and practical problems from diverse industries such as government manufacturing, retail, education, banking/finance, healthcare, and pharmaceutical
Undertake consultancy and industrial projects with significant data analytics component to better understand theoretical concepts from statistics, economics and building future solutions data analytics to impact technological advancements
To be prepared to use advanced analytical tools/decision making tools/operational research techniques to analyse complex problems and develop new such techniques for the future.
To understand cloud computing, it is important to understand the dynamically changing resources and services that are being accessed and the need for the functioning to be organized.
